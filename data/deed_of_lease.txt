2. The Tenant covenants with the Landlord as follows:
(i) TO PAY all taxes, rates, duties, assessments, impositions and outgoings

(ii) TO KEEP the demised premises and any additions thereto and the

determine. THE LANDLORD HEREBY COVENANTS that:
Tenant performing and observing all the other covenants herein contained may
