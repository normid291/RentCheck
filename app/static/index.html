What we check

Two things that matter most.

A clause that takes a right away, and a protection that was never written down.

Flagged clauses

Unfair or non-permitted terms for tenants, ranked by severity.

Example

A landlord may re-enter the property if rent is seven days late, “whether or not lawfully demanded”, with no notice and no court process.

Missing protections

Important clauses that should be there, but are absent from the agreement.

Example

No clause says the caution fee must be receipted, or when and how it will be refunded after you move out.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is this legal advice?

No. Tenura gives you an informational review of common rental practices so you can read an agreement more confidently. It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer.

Which countries are supported?

Nigeria only for now. Tenura applies Nigerian rental norms and asks for the Nigerian state where it matters. More countries are planned.

Is my document stored?

If you analyse without signing in, your document is used to produce the analysis and is not saved. If you are signed in, the analysis and agreement text are saved to your private account so you can reopen them, and you can delete any of them at any time.

Do I need an account?

No. You can run an analysis straight away. An account lets you keep the result and see it again later.
