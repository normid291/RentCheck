2. What RentCheck is — and what it is not

RentCheck reads a tenancy agreement and gives you back:

  • Flagged clauses, each with the words in the agreement that caused the flag.
  • Missing protections — protections a Nigerian tenancy would usually have and this one appears to lack.
  • Questions to ask before you sign.
  • A risk score and a short summary.

It is not legal advice

This matters more than anything else on this page. RentCheck provides general information about common rental practices to help you read a document. It is not legal advice, it is not a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer, and using it does not create a lawyer–client relationship with us.

Some specific consequences:

  • The risk score is a rough indicator, calculated from how many clauses were flagged and how many expected protections were missing. It is not a legal assessment and a low score does not mean an agreement is safe.
  • Flags come from two sources. Ones marked "[flagged by rule]" come from fixed rules in our code and are certain about what they detect. Ones marked "[AI suggestion — worth a manual check]" come from an AI model, are verified only to the extent that the quoted words really appear in your agreement, and may be wrong — either pointing at something that is actually fine, or missing something that is not.
  • Our reference points are general Nigerian rental norms. They may not reflect the law that applies to your specific agreement, the practice in your state, or a recent change in the law.
  • For anything that matters — a large deposit, an unusual term, a dispute with a landlord — speak to a qualified lawyer.

You are responsible for the decision you make about your agreement. We give you a reading aid, not a verdict.
